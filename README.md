I love pins, so I made a mod about 'em!\
Enamel, as said above, is a mod about pins!\
While they may *seem* like a boring trinket, if you put one on, you'll notice that the indicators on your inventory's bottom fill up, and if you reach the limit, you can't equip any more... sad.\
~I've also heard rumours above "overpinning" but, id be safe rather than sorry...~
